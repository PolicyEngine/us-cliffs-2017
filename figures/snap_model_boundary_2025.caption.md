Illustrative model outputs. We calculate 2025 annual resources for a 40-year-old single parent and a 4-year-old dependent in Harris County, Texas. The parent earns $30 per hour over 52 weeks; hours rise with earnings. The parent does not claim cash Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF). Resources exclude health benefits and include noncash food benefits through the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) and the Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC). The [configuration](../examples/snap_model_boundary_zoom_2025.json) records rent, utilities, assets, coverage and other claiming assumptions. We calculate 61 points over $0–$60,000 and 201 points over $33,650–$33,850; lines join calculated points and break across the observed drop. Between $33,726 and $33,727 of earnings, annual resources fall $413.34 and SNAP falls $414.00. Calendar 2025 spans two SNAP fiscal years: January–September uses FY2025 limits, and October–December uses FY2026 limits. At $33,727 of earnings, annual SNAP falls from $597 to $183; the three FY2026 months continue to contribute benefits. The full-range observations also show SNAP reaching zero between $34,000 and $35,000 of earnings. They do not locate that later change at $1 resolution. Texas's Health and Human Services Commission publishes a $2,811 monthly categorical gross-income limit for a two-person household in FY2025; PolicyEngine applies $2,810.50. This model illustration does not establish the exact Texas legal cutoff. [Texas HHSC Bulletin 24-12](https://fhb.hhs.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/mepd-and-twh-Bulletin-24-12.pdf#page=5) supplies the published 165% categorical limit. PolicyEngine 6.19.0 uses US 2.2.1, Core 3.33.3 and spm-calculator 1.0.0 for these calculations.
