Used for: Maine's $9.00 rate. The amended text of 26 MRSA § 664(1) says "Starting January 1, 2017", but the chapter itself took effect 7 January 2017 ("Effective January 7, 2017."), so $9.00 could apply only from 7 January; $7.50 applied before.

CHAPTER 2
I.B. 4 - L.D. 1661

starting January 1, 2018, the minimum hourly wage is

per hour. On January 1, 2021 and each January 1st
